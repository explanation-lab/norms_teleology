NORMS TELEOLOGY — SHAUN REVISIONS REVIEW
Version: norms-teleology-single-animal-pilot-1.1.0

Extract the whole ZIP and double-click index.html. Keep every folder together.
No installation, server, internet or sign-in is needed. JavaScript must be on.

The reviewer panel can select any condition and animal (nine combinations),
jump to any screen, reveal explanation boxes, shuffle question order, restore
canonical order, and download local review responses. Try participant flow
opens participant.html without reviewer controls.

Each participant receives ONE benefit condition and ONE independently selected
animal, sampled uniformly from Virdex, Nalk and Marvin. Five questions are
randomized once per participant. Condition, animal and question order survive
reloads. Every rating reveals a required explanation box before Continue can
advance. The seven-point scale is identical for all questions. Story and images
stay above each question. Optional demographics follow the five questions.

CHANGES FROM THE APPROVED 1.0.0 REVIEW
- Survival wording now describes a benefit to the individual focal animal.
- Nalk ecology describes fish receiving oxygen instead of snails.
- Control passages consistently describe no significant effects on the focal
  animal or other animals in the area; the mild-scent, harmless-bubble and
  blue-grass details were removed.
- Marvin ecology now explicitly describes finches eating the exposed seeds.
- The significance question now says diminished instead of missing, for all
  three animals. All other rating statements, including value, are unchanged.
- Retained the approved Marvin hoof setup, captions and matched images. The
  document's older front-claw setup was not restored.
- Corrected the duplicated more in the Virdex survival sentence and normalized
  sentence punctuation; tracked deletions and editorial labels are not shown.

Consent, concise instructions, explanations, demographics, random assignment,
question randomization, all six images and the reviewer panel are retained.
JSON and CSV exports record condition, selected_animal_id, item_id, measure,
question order, rating and explanation. Exports are marked review/local_preview.

STATUS
Offline review only. This revision has not been registered or deployed to the
Study Console. The existing live 1.0.0 study and its 225-participant target are
unchanged. Review responses are never submitted to the lab or to Prolific.
