# Revised mixed pilot 1.0.3

Every participant receives Virdexes, Nalks, and Marvins once each, with one control,
one survival-benefit, and one ecological-benefit case. Six balanced animal-condition
pairings are assigned by the Console. Independently randomized animal order and
one shared randomized five-question order persist on reload.

Cases follow the revised November 2 materials. Five measures per animal are
ought_to_have, wrongness, special_importance, value, and meant_to_do. Each uses
one seven-point agreement rating without explanation. Nalk DVs omit dense;
importance uses Having spots/hair/spikes makes [animal] especially important
creatures. Value statements use the short feature name; all exact text is in
js/materials.js. Approved paired images remain above each question; Marvin
hoof spikes were darkened November 5 for visibility.

Consent, instructions, three story screens, 15 ratings, and optional demographics
produce 21 raw trial records. Collection saves only complete consented sessions,
with explicit pairing and per-animal benefit_condition fields. Reviewer and local
participant previews never submit data. The separate frozen Console release is
registered as NORMS-TELEOLOGY-REVISED-MIXE-8ES4 with 50 participants total. See
../../../docs/MIXED_REVISED_PILOT_CONSOLE.md for verification and final Prolific steps.
