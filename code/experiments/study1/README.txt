NORMS TELEOLOGY — STUDY 1

To review
1. Extract the entire ZIP first. Keep its folders together.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens the study in your browser.
3. Use Condition to switch between Control, Survival benefit, and Ecological benefit.
4. Use Screen to jump directly to consent, instructions, the story, any of the four
   questions, demographics, or the final screen. Changes take effect immediately.
5. "Show explanation box" lets you inspect a question's text box without answering.
6. "Try participant flow" opens the study without review controls in another tab.

No installation, server, sign-in, internet connection, or build step is required.
JavaScript must be enabled. Review responses are never transmitted. If browser
storage is blocked, navigation still works, but answers may not survive a jump.

Participant sequence
Consent -> Instructions -> Story and pictures -> Four rating/explanation pairs
-> Demographics -> Thank you.

The story and images remain above each question. Selecting a rating reveals the
explanation box. A rating and a nonblank explanation are needed to Continue.
All questions use the identical 1–7 agreement scale with the same seven labels.
The final form uses the Purpose Norms demographic fields: age (18–100), gender,
race and ethnicity, with Other text entries for gender and race. Every demographic
item is optional. The standalone feedback form has been removed.

Review terms
The consent form follows the causal-verbs cross-cultural study. Duration is an
initial estimate of about 5 minutes; confirm it through piloting. Payment is
described by the recruitment listing rather than inventing a payment amount.

Data and upload
This review ZIP is for review and sharing. index.html always shows the reviewer
panel; participant.html is a local participant preview. Neither submits data.
JSON and CSV exports label all responses as review/local preview data.
JSON includes the demographic answers; a separate demographic CSV is also available.

The separate norms-teleology-study1-upload.zip has a Console-connected index.html,
review.html for the reviewer panel, and participant.html for a local flow preview.
It includes the frozen official client and machine-readable study configuration.
Its collection index.html requires hosting and a real Console launch link; it is
not the double-click review entry. Complete hosting, Console registration, Sandbox
verification, recruitment terms, sample size and Prolific setup before collection.
The repository remains private. This package does not launch recruitment.
