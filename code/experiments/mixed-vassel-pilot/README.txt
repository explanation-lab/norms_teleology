NORMS TELEOLOGY — VASSEL MIXED-CONDITION PILOT
Version: norms-teleology-mixed-vassel-pilot-1.0.1

Extract the whole ZIP, keep the folders together, and double-click index.html.
No server, installation, sign-in or internet connection is needed.
Reviewer controls select all six animal-condition pairings, animals and screens,
shuffle animal or question order, restore review order, and export local answers.
Try participant flow opens participant.html without reviewer controls.

Each participant sees Virdexes, Nalks, and Vassels once each and receives control,
survival benefit and ecological benefit exactly once. Pairing and animal order
are randomized independently. A single randomized order of the SIX questions
is reused for each animal. Assignments, orders and answers persist on reload.

Vassels replace Marvins. The Vassel case concerns cup-shaped shoulder scales
collecting rainwater. Survival and ecological passages apply Shaun's November 9
replacements, all ending in making it/them more likely to survive. The obvious
Virdex typo making it is was corrected to making it. In control, the collected water evaporates without significant effects
on the vassel or other animals. Two matched images compare the endpoints of the
scale feature: cup-shaped scales present versus absent, following the existing
study's image-pair format. There is no added continuum-rating task.

The six measures are ought to have, something wrong, special importance, special
value, meant to do, and reason to exist. The new question reads, for example:
The spots on Virdexes are a good reason for Virdexes to exist.
Questions use the matching species and feature. Nalk wording omits dense in DVs;
value and importance retain the approved short feature names. All responses use
the same fully labeled seven-point agreement scale. No explanations are asked.
The story and both images stay above each question. Consent, concise instructions
and optional final demographics follow the approved study.

Each complete session has 18 ratings and 24 raw trial records. Exports include
pairing_id, actual per-animal benefit_condition, item_order and question_order.
All reviewer/local responses are marked invalid for actual participant data.
