# Vassel mixed pilot 1.0.1

Virdexes, Nalks and Vassels replace the previous Virdex/Nalk/Marvin set. Each
participant receives every animal and every benefit condition exactly once.
One of six pairings, an independent animal order, and a single six-question order
are randomized. The question order repeats across animals for that participant.

Vassels are arboreal animals with small cup-shaped shoulder scales that collect
rainwater. Survival benefits the individual vassel; ecology benefits nearby tree
salamanders. In control, water evaporates without significant effects on either.
Two matched images retain the existing feature-present/feature-absent comparison.

Measures: ought_to_have, wrongness, special_importance, value, meant_to_do,
reason_to_exist. Exact text, images and all three passages are in js/materials.js.
Six questions per animal produce 18 ratings and 24 raw trial records. No written
explanations. The same consent, instructions and optional demographics are retained.
The reviewer is offline only. A separate frozen collection release is registered
as NORMS-TELEOLOGY-VASSEL-MIXED-JDTA, with 50 participants total. Six hosted Sandbox
sessions and the export audit passed November 10. See ../../../docs/VASSEL_PILOT_CONSOLE.md
for the remaining Prolific steps.

November 9 wording revision: the six survival/ecology passages use the uniform
ending making it/them more likely to survive. Nalk passages include the additional
use-this-oxygen sentence proposed by Shaun; Vassel survival uses a vassel.
The Virdex making it is typo was corrected to making it. Controls and all other
materials, questions, images and design are retained.
